Volume Settlement Method

  1. Settlement Cycle: Monthly volume spans from the day following the prior month's cutoff date through the current month's cutoff date. Cutoffs adjust for national holidays or emergencies as announced by operational centers.
  2. Senior Volume Deferral: Pearl Masters and above who require supplemental volume after the cutoff date must submit a Rank Restock & Review Application online or in-center prior to the cutoff date. Unregistered requests cannot be accommodated.
  3. Inquiries: Detailed restock and deferral guidelines are available through local operational centers and customer care.

Bonus Payout Method

  1. Payout Schedule: Monthly bonuses are remitted on the 15th of each month directly into the member's registered bank account, deferring to the next business day if falling on holidays.
  2. Remittance Fees: Wire transfer fees are deducted per transaction (Cooperative Bank, Chang Hwa Bank, Hua Nan Bank, and UOB Bank enjoy zero fees; other banks incur standard NT$ 9 / RM 0.1 fees).
  3. Clawback Rights: The company retains a 2-year retroactive recovery right from the date of disbursement for any erroneous overpayments.

Autonomous Branch ‧ Independent Growth

State 1: Standard Qualified Master (Downline Qualifies Independently)

Qualified Master Line +1 6% Leadership Bonus
What happened with the downline partner?
  • Personal Volume: Reached ≥ 160 SV in monthly personal purchases.
  • Group Volume: Personal group (including downline non-Masters) reached ≥ 3,200 SV.
  • Status: Qualifies as an autonomous Master and receives their own dual global pool bonuses!
How does this benefit you as the upline?
  • Key Advancement Pillar: Counts as +1 Active Qualified Master Line, advancing your rank toward Silver, Gold, Pearl, or Blue Diamond.
  • Pipeline Activated: Their group decouples from your personal group, qualifying you to earn 6% Leadership Bonus on their volume!
  • Partner Tip: You must maintain your own 3,200 SV group volume to claim both Qualified Team and Leadership Bonuses!
Dynamic Compression ‧ Zero Wasted Volume

State 2: Non-Qualified Master (Group Volume Compresses Upward to You)

Not Counted as Qualified Line Fulfills Your 3,200 SV Quota
What happened with the downline partner?
  • Personal Volume: Reached ≥ 160 SV in personal purchases.
  • Group Volume: Total group volume fell short of the threshold (< 3,200 SV).
  • Status: Did not qualify independently this month; cannot claim the dual global pool bonuses.
How does this benefit you as the upline?
  • Zero Waste: Under Dynamic Compression, their entire group volume compresses upward into your Merged Group Volume!
  • Meeting Your Quota: Their volume helps satisfy your 3,200 SV responsibility, securing your Qualified Master Bonus (NT$ 7,000).
  • Partner Tip: Because this Master was not qualified, this line cannot be counted as an active qualified line this month.
Official Penetration Rule ‧ Maximum Benefit

State 3: Zero-Volume Special Master (Downline Volume Penetrates into Your Personal Group!)

Penetrates to Personal Group Unlocks 10% Team Bonus
What happened with the downline partner?
  • Personal Volume: Personal volume is precisely zero (= 0 SV).
  • Downline Structure: Non-Master members, Associates, or Senior Associates placed beneath them generated orders (e.g., 2,000 SV).
  • Official Clause: Company manual states: "If a Master has zero personal volume, non-Master downline volume rolls up into the upline Master's personal group."
How does this benefit you as the upline?
  • Direct Penetration: Non-Master volume bypasses this Master and counts directly toward your Personal Group Volume!
  • Unlocks Team Bonus: This volume easily pushes your personal group past 3,200 SV, allowing you to claim the NT$ 12,000 Qualified Team Bonus!
  • Partner Tip: This is a major official bonus incentive for active upline leaders—make sure you understand its mechanics!
Low-Volume Compression ‧ Supports Merged Quota

State 4: Insufficient Quota Master (Personal Volume 1 ~ 159 SV)

Excluded from 0 SV Penetration Compresses to Merged Group
What happened with the downline partner?
  • Personal Volume: Personal volume is between 1 ~ 159 SV (just short of the 160 SV threshold).
  • Crucial Distinction: Because personal volume is greater than 0 SV, the "0 SV Penetration Rule" cannot apply.
  • Status: Missed the personal quota threshold; cannot receive bonuses for this month.
How does this benefit you as the upline?
  • Standard Compression: Their entire group volume rolls up into your Merged Group Volume to support your 3,200 SV requirement.
  • No Team Bonus: This volume does not enter your personal group, so it cannot be used to claim the 10% Qualified Team Bonus.
  • Coaching Tip: If a partner is only a few SV away from 160, remind them to top up so they can receive their personal rebate!
Executive Privilege ‧ Focus on Leadership

State 5: Automatic Rescue 5th Leg (Eliminates Retail Group Pressure!)

5th Leg Automatically Offsets Team Bonus Excluded

  • All Senior Bonuses Intact: Fully preserves your qualifications for Leadership Bonus, Dividend Bonus, and annual awards.

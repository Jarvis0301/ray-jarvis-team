Sales Settlement Regulations

  1. Settlement Cycle: Monthly performance is calculated from the day following the previous cut-off date through the current cut-off date. Adjustments may occur during public holidays. Official dates are published via UVACO service centers.
  2. Deferred Performance Form: Pearl Masters and above who need to supplement sales volume post-deadline must submit the official "Rank Adjustment & Verification Form" prior to the cut-off date. Late requests cannot be processed.
  3. Support Channels: Please contact regional Operations Centers or Customer Service for assistance.

Bonus Disbursal Terms

  1. Disbursal Date & Channel: Bonuses are disbursed directly via bank transfer on the 15th of each month into the Distributor’s registered financial account.
  2. Transfer Handling Fees: Bank wire fees apply per disbursement (Taiwan Cooperative Bank, Chang Hwa Bank, Hua Nan Bank, and UOB Bank are exempt; other banking institutions incur standard fees of NT$ 9 / RM 0.1).
  3. Overpayment Clawback: UVACO reserves the right to recover any overpaid bonuses within a two-year statutory period from the payment date.
